The bike

The machine
that gets me there.

I don't need the lightest bike, the newest bike or the bike everyone on the internet says I should have. I need a bike I trust when the road gets long, the weather turns bad and the next town is a long way away.

Why this one

Built for
being away.

This is the bike I keep coming back to for long trips. A custom titanium setup, built around the idea that the less I have to worry about the machine, the more attention I can give to the place I'm riding through.

It carries everything I need without pretending I don't need anything. Bags, water, tools, food, cameras, a bit of comfort. Enough to live on the road, not enough to turn the road into a mobile garage.

The point isn't the bike.
It's what the bike lets me do.

About

I'm Adam.

A long time at sea.
A bicycle. A lot of questions.

I’ve spent most of my adult life working on boats. Yachting has given me a bloody good life in a lot of ways. It’s also taken up an enormous amount of it.

For years, when work got too much, I’d leave, get on a bicycle and disappear for a while. Somewhere along the way I realised those trips weren’t just holidays.

They were the bits of my life where I felt most like myself.

So this is an experiment.

I’m riding because I love riding. I’m travelling because I want to see what’s on the other side of the next hill. And I’m writing because I’m trying to make sense of what comes next.

There’s no grand plan here. No ten-step system. No promise that I’m going to figure everything out.

Just a man trying to fall back in love with life, while working out who the hell he wants to be.
